We believe that geography should not dictate destiny. Our mission is to deliver the critical infrastructure of education, nutrition, and healthcare to India's most remote communities.

Our Impact Journey

How we transform communities.

Sustainable change happens step by step. Explore our approach to empowering rural India.

The foundation of every dream.

In rural Palghar, a simple notebook can be the difference between dropping out and graduating. Through Project Akshar, we distribute comprehensive literacy kits — and run after-school digital labs — in 450+ remote tribal schools.

₹500 Cost per kit
12,847 Students reached

You can't learn on an empty stomach.

Education is futile without nutrition. Our Nourish initiative operates 12 high-capacity community kitchens, ensuring daily hot, protein-rich meals for day-laborers and their children across Palghar, Raigad, and Nashik districts.

₹50 Cost per meal
2.1M Meals served

Healing where it's needed most.

For many villages, the nearest hospital is a day's journey. Sanjeevani deploys mobile medical clinics and surgical camps to treat preventable diseases — cataracts, malaria, malnutrition — directly in the community.

15,312 Patients treated
43 Villages covered
