Education Palghar District

How Meera became the first graduate in her village

April 2024 4 min read

Meera was born into a family of agricultural laborers in the drought-prone Palghar district. When she was 12, she was expected to drop out of school to help her parents in the fields, just like her older sisters.

"I used to hide my books under my mattress. I knew that if I stopped reading, I would forget the letters."

The turning point came when HopeBridge's Project Akshar arrived in her village. Through consistent counseling with her parents and the provision of a full literacy kit, uniform, and bicycle, Meera was able to continue her education at the secondary school 8 kilometers away.

Today, Meera has passed her Class 12 board exams with an 88% distinction, becoming the first person—man or woman—in her village to achieve this milestone. She now plans to pursue nursing.
